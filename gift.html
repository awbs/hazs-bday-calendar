The cabin

The Duck's Den is a reclaimed-timber cabin tucked into a clearing in the trees at Woodfield Farm, with a natural swimming lake a short stroll from its front steps. Inside it's open plan, with a super king-size bed that looks out into the woods, and a covered front deck with a wood-burning stove for the evenings.

  • Bedroom: one super king-size bed, with bed linen and towels provided.
  • Kitchen: two-ring hob, oven, toaster, kettle and undercounter fridge, with cooking basics supplied.
  • Bathroom: hot shower, sink and toilet in the cabin.
  • Bathhouse: a separate bathing cabin with a roll-top bath and a cold shower.
  • Cosy bits: wood burner on the deck, radiators and air conditioning.
  • Indoors: board games, books, TV, Bluetooth speaker and wifi.

Things to do there

  • Wild swimming lake, with a rowing boat and a stand-up paddleboard to borrow.
  • Lakeside sauna, with a private session included in the stay.
  • Tennis court, free to use.
  • Firepit and BBQ for dinner outdoors.
  • Woodland all around, with deer and rabbits for neighbours.

Nearby

  • Charlecote Park (2.5 miles): National Trust estate with a deer herd.
  • The Fox at Loxley (3 miles): country pub.
  • Loxley Viewpoint (5 miles): panoramic views.
  • Stratford-upon-Avon (8 miles) and Warwick (8.4 miles).
  • Chipping Campden (14 miles): Cotswold town.
  • Farm shops: Cobbs, Daylesford Organic and Charlotte's Garden Centre.

What to pack

  • Walking boots for the woods.
  • Swimming things and flip-flops for the lake.
  • A bathrobe for the sauna.
